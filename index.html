Help

  • Pick a piece in the palette, then tap the canvas — the piece is placed immediately and snaps to nearby track ends (dots turn green when connected). On desktop a translucent preview follows your cursor.
  • Keep dragging before you release to fine-position the piece with live snapping.
  • After placing, the Move tool is active with the new piece selected: drag it to adjust, tap empty space to deselect. Hold Shift on release to keep the same piece armed for rapid placing.
  • Chain pieces: re-pick the piece and tap/press near the end of the track — it snaps on.
  • Pan tool: drag to look around without placing. Fit zooms to fit the whole track. Two fingers → pan & pinch-zoom.
  • Rotate left/right rotates by 45° — while placing they turn the ghost; with Move they rotate the selection.
  • Move: drag on empty space to rubber-band select · ctrl/cmd+click a piece to add/remove it · drag a selected piece to move the whole group (it snaps) · the rotate buttons turn the selection.
  • Delete: tap a piece to remove it. The three-dot tool taps to cycle its color.
  • Close the loop (Menu or L): select exactly two pieces of the same lane count with Move and the gap between their free ends is filled with the shortest run of that family's straights and 45° corners that misses every placed piece. Ends 75 mm apart in level bridge straight over; smaller gaps and same-level overlaps are avoided. If it can't close, the tool says why and offers to remove pieces until it can — one undo puts everything back.
  • Scale: 1 px = 1 cm, one grid square = 1 m (same as original).

Keyboard (desktop)

  • 1–9 pieces · Space pan · Q/W/E select/delete/color
  • Z/X rotate · R undo · F fit · +/− zoom · Shift+drag pan · L close loop
  • PageUp/PageDown (or the level buttons) raise/lower the armed piece or selection by 10 mm — or just chain off a slope and the level is adopted automatically. The lowest piece is always floor level (0 mm); pieces above it show their level, slopes show an amber chevron at the high end, and banks don’t change elevation.
  • Del delete selection · Esc pan tool (dismisses armed piece / selection)

Complete track

Tap two end pieces to complete the track — the gap is filled with the shortest run of straights and 45° corners that clears every piece. If no workable solution is found, the tool can automatically remove pieces one at a time until the track can be completed. Both ends must be of the same lane count and elevation, with exactly one free end each. Straights, corners, waves and lane changers are supported as ends.

Publish

Publishing saves this track to the server under a name; from then on your edits save automatically. Unpublished tracks stay on this device only.

Optional: name and passphrase

Empty: the track publishes without a byline. The track name above is not affected.

Use a passphrase when you want to be the only one who can save over this track: a finished layout you will maintain, or tracks published under your name.

Skip it when others may edit the track directly. Signed tracks can still be copied by anyone.

A name alone, e.g. Alex, is a byline anyone can type. Add # and a phrase, e.g. Alex#fluffy track 42, and the track is signed: it shows as Alex!9f3ab2c1, and only the same phrase can save over it or restore old versions.

The same name and phrase on another device gives the same Alex!9f3ab2c1. The browser remembers the phrase; there is no account and no reset. If the phrase is lost, the track stays as published.
