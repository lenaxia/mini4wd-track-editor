# 0006 — Close the loop: solver-driven gap closing between two selected pieces

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Triggered by:** Owner request — after free-building with S-turns and mixed
sections, tracks often end with ends that cannot complete. Wanted a tool that
selects two pieces and fills the gap between them with a minimal, optimal run,
taking elevation and obstacles into account. Scope agreed: 3-lane straights,
45° corners, lane changer for testing.

---

## Summary

New `src/solver.js`: an A\* router over (x, y, heading, level) states.
Transitions are the allowed pieces traversed in **either vertex order** — a
corner entered at v1 and exited at v0 turns the opposite way, which is how
right turns exist at all in a 45°-grid world (the tangent helpers already
model this). Because every joint in a snapped track is exact, endpoints live
on the piece lattice generated by chord displacements; for a single connected
component whose pieces are all in the solver set, a closing run always exists
(the component itself realizes the displacement). Cost is lap length (admissible
heuristic = crow-flight distance), tie-broken by piece count.

Collision is **road-level, not footprint-level**: straights are their exact
OBB (SAT with the 1 cm inset, mirroring `store.bboxOverlap` semantics);
corner/hairpin roads are the solved arc centerline sampled ~15° and inflated
by band/2. Chained corners genuinely overlap as squares but not as roads —
a footprint test would reject every legitimate 90° turn. Pieces sharing a
connection vertex are exempt (joints). Plan overlap at a level gap ≥ 75 mm is
a bridge, not a clash (matches the `_over`/`_warn` standard).

Three outcomes:
1. **Exact** — lands on the goal vertex within 0.01 cm / 0.05°, joints perfect.
2. **Flex** — off-grid ends (e.g. two separately-built clusters): best landing
   within SNAP_RADIUS, then the final piece is re-welded onto the target with
   `snapPiece` — identical semantics to a manual drag; the previous joint
   absorbs the remainder and the toast reports the bend size.
3. **Honest failure** — `level` (different levels, flat-only set), `no-open`,
   or `no-path` with the nearest-fit miss (cm / °) in the toast.

Known honest limitation (documented by a unit test): a same-level wall fully
crossing a 36-wide corridor is *unclosable* — detours need exact lattice
returns whose diagonal parts (√2 components) must cancel, and the minimal
cancelling construction (an 8-corner octagon, net displacement exactly 0) is
~10 m and rarely fits. The tool reports `no-path` rather than returning an
overlapping path — the invariant the tests pin.

## Changes

- `src/solver.js` (new): road models, collision (`piecesCollide` exported),
  A\* with shared expansion budget across open-vertex combos (nearest combo
  first, others skipped when they cannot beat a found solution), adaptive
  cost cap (10 m + 8× crow flight) so no-solution cases exhaust in ~1 s.
- `src/store.js`: `addPieces(list)` — one history step, run ends selected.
- `src/ui.js`: `closeLoopAction()` + ☰ → "Close loop (2 selected pieces)".
- `src/input.js`: `L` key.
- `index.html`: menu button, help entries, cache-buster v23.
- `tests/unit/solver.test.js` (new, 14 tests), store test for `addPieces`,
  `tests/e2e/closeloop.spec.js` (new, 2 specs).

## Owner-facing behavior

Select exactly two pieces (✥ + ctrl/tap-toggle), press **L** or ☰ → Close
loop. Toast reports pieces + meters; flex bends are reported in cm. Undo (R)
removes the whole run in one step.
